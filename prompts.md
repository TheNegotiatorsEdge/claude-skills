# MAGNETIC Influence — AI Prompt Library

Practical prompts from *Magnetic Influence* for preparing deals and conversations. Use these
when the user wants to **prepare** — research, rewrite, design options, profile a counterpart,
or train a skill — rather than be scored. Adapt each prompt to the user's specifics; don't paste
raw. Always keep the framework's spirit: principled, trust-first, value-creating.

---

## How to write a high-quality prompt (the book's structure)

Every strong prompt the user runs should include four parts:

1. **Character** — tell the AI who it is. Example: *"You are an expert negotiator who follows Tim
   Castle's Magnetic Influence frameworks and principles, with 20 years of experience doing
   high-quality deals that create value and expand the size of the pie."*
2. **Objective** — the specific problem to solve.
3. **Do's and Don'ts** — the guardrails. E.g. *Do:* lead with "so what," not methodology; match
   tone to the audience (a board wants risk flags, a client wants value, a team wants next
   steps); keep it to 3–5 sentences ready to paste. *Don't:* write like a report; guess; use
   jargon; sugar-coat.
4. **Output** — the exact format wanted (bullet points, a short paragraph, a word limit, three
   findings, a 3–5 sentence narrative for a named audience).

Clearer input = higher-quality output.

## Prompts by principle

### Meaningful Impact
- "Identify areas where we could create more value for both sides."

### Authenticity (creative deal design)
- "Based on the alternative options I have and what's been proposed, create ten alternative deal
  structures where both parties achieve incremental value." Then add your own flair — bundles,
  licensing plus consulting, risk-sharing, value tiers (not discounts) — reflecting your values.

### G³ — Gravitas, Generosity, Gratitude
- "Rewrite my negotiation email to convey authority, calm confidence, and strategic clarity
  without sounding aggressive or defensive."
- "Give me language that positions me as the trusted advisor rather than the seller."
- "Suggest ways I can create value beyond the transaction so the other party feels genuinely
  supported."
- "Rewrite this email so it emphasizes partnership, shared wins, and expansion of the pie."
- "Help me express gratitude in a way that feels sincere, professional, and influence-enhancing."
- "Turn this recap note into a moment of acknowledgement that leaves them feeling seen and
  valued."

> Gratitude caution: in purely distributive, one-off price negotiations, don't reflexively say
> "thank you" — it can trigger exploitation. Gratitude shines in cooperative, ongoing contexts.

### Networking Super Connector
- "Rewrite my introduction so it is memorable, warm, and positions me as a high-value connection."
- "Help me craft a 20-second elevator pitch that sparks curiosity and invites conversation."

### Energetic Vibration
- "What emotions is this person likely experiencing?"
- "What pressures might they be under?"
- "Rewrite my response using a high-empathy approach that acknowledges their concerns and
  validates their perspective while maintaining my position with clarity and warmth."

### Trust
- "Highlight my emotional triggers, blind spots, and biases."
- "How can I express this with more empathy, trust-building, and clarity?"
- "Based on this client conversation, what would make them feel seen and respected and
  demonstrate empathy and kindness?"
- "Give me 10 trust-building phrases I can use when price objections are raised."

Pre-conversation research prompts (walk in as the expert): ask AI to surface headwinds and
tailwinds, challenge your assumptions, expose your blind spots, find areas of value, and
stress-test and fact-check — so you arrive as a consultant, not a vendor.

### Intuition
- "Create a daily intuition training that incorporates a 3–5 minute meditation, coaches me on
  bringing awareness to the energy of others, and helps me take one intuitive action."

### Connection
- Lead with genuine, curious questions. Ask AI to help you design open questions that make the
  other person feel seen and build psychological safety, rather than questions that interrogate
  or steer.

## The TRUST AI™ framework

A quick checklist for using AI to build trust on any deal.

| Principle | AI function | Trust benefit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T**ransparency | Summarize deal terms and surface key points. | Openness; shared information. |
| **R**espect | Personalize the deal and conversation to behavioral preferences. | People feel seen and valued. |
| **U**nderstanding | Feed AI what you know about the counterparty and ask it to run a DISC behavioral read — outgoing vs. reserved, task- vs. people-oriented, Dominant / Influencer / Steadiness / Compliance. | Boosts emotional intelligence. |
| **S**tewardship | Ask AI to help conduct bias audits. | Demonstrates integrity and moral compass. |
| **T**rack record | Ask AI to find trends in your past deals — what led to wins, which concessions helped. | Builds consistency. |

## Reminder on AI's role

In every prompt above, AI is a thought-starter and preparation partner — it expands options,
surfaces blind spots, and sharpens language at speed. It does **not** do the influencing for the
user. The humanity, judgment, intuition, and genuine care are theirs; that is the part that is
magnetic.
