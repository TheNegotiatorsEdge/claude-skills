A coaching skill built on the MAGNETIC Influence framework from Tim Castle's book Magnetic Influence. Bring it any real situation—a negotiation, a pitch, a high-stakes email, a job offer, a difficult conversation—and Claude assesses it across eight principles (Meaningful Impact, Authenticity, Gravitas/Generosity/Gratitude, Networking, Energetic Vibration, Trust, Intuition, Connection), identifies what's weak, and returns specific guidance: your strengths, your risks, your best next move, a line to say, and a line to avoid. Built for principled, trust-based influence—never manipulation or pressure tactics.
